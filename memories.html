Memories

Little Moments, Beautiful Memories 📸

Some moments are big celebrations. Others last only a few seconds. Here are a few special pieces of Osiris’s story worth remembering.

Featured memory

Those First Steps 👣

First came those careful little steps. One step became another, confidence grew, and before long Osiris had discovered a completely new way to explore his world.

A small step became the beginning of a much bigger world.

Five special memories

Mama & Papa ❤️

Of all the sounds Osiris was learning to make, two became particularly special: Mama and Papa. Simple little words, but unforgettable ones for the people hearing them.

Little Namaste 🙏

Two little hands together. Namaste. A beautiful little connection between Osiris and the Nepali culture surrounding him.

My Nepal Adventure 🇳🇵

A journey filled with family, culture and new surroundings, including the mountain experience of Ghandruk and a meaningful traditional rice feeding ceremony.

My First Birthday 🎂

Balloons, a smash cake, play and plenty to explore. A special celebration for one very curious little boy.

The days move quickly, but the memories stay. ❤️
