About Osiris

Meet Osiris 👋

A lovable, energetic little boy with a curious mind, an adventurous spirit and a wonderfully cute personality.

This is me.

Osiris is energetic, curious, observant and independent. New places, faces and unfamiliar objects get his attention quickly, but he likes to take his time and watch before deciding he is ready to join in.

With new people, he is not immediately social. Give him some time, play with him and make him laugh, and his friendly, playful personality begins to appear.

What makes me laugh?

Tickles & pretend sneezes.

A little tickle on the belly is usually enough. So is a dramatic fake sneeze. And once Osiris starts laughing, the funny, lovely sound can make everyone else smile too.

My roots

Nepal 🇳🇵 + Australia 🇦🇺

Osiris is growing up surrounded by Nepali culture, family traditions, language, food and celebrations while experiencing everyday life in Australia. Nepal became part of his story early, including a meaningful traditional rice feeding ceremony surrounded by family and culture.
